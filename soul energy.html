SOUL ENERGY & SOULS

SOULS • SOUL ENERGY • LIVING THINGS

OVERVIEW

Souls and Soul Energy are fundamental components of life within the Barrens. Every living thing possesses a soul, while Soul Energy exists within living beings and serves as the source of supernatural abilities known as Gifts.

Although souls cannot normally be seen or physically interacted with, they can be damaged, altered, or even removed. Much about their true nature remains unknown.

SOULS

WHAT IS A SOUL?

Every living thing possesses a soul.

A soul serves as both the source of an individual's autonomy and the tether connecting them to their physical body.

The soul is responsible for maintaining the connection between a living being and its physical form.

SOUL DAMAGE & ALTERATION

Souls are not indestructible. They can be damaged or altered, although the exact effects depend on the nature and severity of the damage.

Unlike ordinary physical injuries, damage to the soul can affect the fundamental connection between an individual and their body.

SOUL REMOVAL

A soul can, under certain circumstances, be completely removed from a physical body.

When this occurs, the body remains physically intact but becomes little more than a husk of its former self.

The individual is no longer truly themselves.

SOULS AFTER DEATH

When a living being dies, its soul leaves the physical body and vanishes.

What happens afterward is unknown.

Whether souls drift somewhere within the Barrens, return to the realm in another form, reincarnate as something else, or cease to exist entirely remains unknown.

SOUL ENERGY

WHAT IS SOUL ENERGY?

Soul Energy, commonly abbreviated as SE, is an energy associated with the soul.

SE exists within living beings and is the fundamental source of Gifts.

Generally, the greater an individual's Soul Energy, the greater their potential for supernatural ability.

MEASURING SOUL ENERGY

Soul Energy is measured in SU, or Soul Units.

TOTAL SE The total amount of Soul Energy an individual possesses.
TAPPED SE The amount of Soul Energy an individual is currently accessing or utilizing.
100,000 SU
GIFT THRESHOLD

Individuals possessing more than 100,000 SU have a Gift.

SOULS & SE IN LIVING THINGS

HUMANS

All humans possess souls and Soul Energy.

Humans do not possess physical Soul Cores. Instead, their soul and Soul Energy exist as invisible phenomena throughout the body.

The soul maintains the connection between the person's physical body and their consciousness, while Soul Energy provides the foundation for Gifts.

VOIDSPAWN & SOUL CORES

Voidspawn possess souls and Soul Energy, but their souls function differently from those of humans.

Instead of existing invisibly throughout their bodies, Voidspawn possess a physical Soul Core.

A Soul Core acts as the central vessel of the Voidspawn's soul and cannot normally be destroyed by ordinary people.

RECONSTITUTION

When a Voidspawn's physical body dies, its Soul Core separates from the body and drifts away to an unknown location.

Eventually, the Voidspawn reconstitutes a new physical body.

The reconstituted Voidspawn does not retain its previous memories.

In extremely rare cases, however, a Voidspawn may retain fragments or portions of its former memories.
